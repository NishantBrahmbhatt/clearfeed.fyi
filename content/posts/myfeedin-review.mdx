## What is MyFeedIn and what does it do?

You build **named feeds** (prospects, peers, creators, whatever language you use). The extension shows **only** those people’s posts, in a calm surface inside LinkedIn.

Around that core you get: analytics on your own posts, analytics on which comments moved profile visits, and **focus mode** that blocks the main feed when you want guardrails.

It is built for people who treat LinkedIn as **work**, not cable TV.

## What are MyFeedIn's limitations?

**Desktop jail.** Phone-first LinkedIn? This is not your tool. Say that plainly so nobody wastes an afternoon hoping.

**LinkedIn UI risk.** Browser extensions ride on whatever LinkedIn ships Tuesday. Breakages happen; fixes tend to follow. Know you are on someone else’s moving sidewalk.

**Analytics depth.** Enough for most operators. Not a Shield replacement if you live in charts.
