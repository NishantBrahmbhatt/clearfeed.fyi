## What are the most effective LinkedIn productivity habits?

**Batch writing** once a week. Sixty to ninety minutes beats seven tortured evenings.

**Draft off-platform.** Notes, Docs, anything that does not nag you with red badges.

**Timer on before the tab opens.** Fifteen minutes for comments. Buzzer means go do your life.

**Notifications almost off.** DMs and connection requests stay. The rest are booby traps.

**Desktop-first.** Mobile wants “just checks.” Desktop wants work.

**Batch comment replies** on your own posts. Once a day beats living in the thread.

## How do you build a sustainable LinkedIn routine that fits around a full-time job?

**Monday:** write.

**Tue–Thu:** post (twice across the week is fine) + fifteen minutes on your MyFeedIn list.

**Friday:** five minutes to refill ideas and notice what landed.

**Weekend:** touch grass. Audiences are thinner; guilt is optional.

Total: roughly **two hours a week** if you batch, plus small daily engagement stamps.

## What LinkedIn features are worth using and which are worth ignoring?

**Use:** custom lists via MyFeedIn, native scheduler when it helps, Following tab if you want a lighter native view, monthly analytics glance, DMs for real humans.

**Skip for most creators:** Stories (where dead), heavy Live unless you love production, Newsletter until you have a base, random Events, and the main feed once you have a list workflow.
