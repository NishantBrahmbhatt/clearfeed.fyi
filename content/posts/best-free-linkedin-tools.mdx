## Feed management and engagement

### MyFeedIn — free custom LinkedIn feeds
**Free plan: one custom feed, up to 10 people**

[MyFeedIn](https://myfeedin.co?atp=PO9qAX) solves the most common LinkedIn productivity problem: the algorithm filling your feed with people you never chose to follow. The free plan lets you build one custom feed of up to 10 specific LinkedIn users. Their posts appear in a clean, distraction-free list inside LinkedIn with no algorithm involvement. You see only who you chose.

It also includes LinkedIn's feed blocking focus mode and basic post analytics. For anyone who wants to engage deliberately rather than reactively, this is the most important free tool on this list.

**Best for:** Anyone who loses time to LinkedIn's main feed and wants to replace it with a curated list of specific people.

**Get it:** [myfeedin.co](https://myfeedin.co?atp=PO9qAX) — free, Chrome only, no credit card required.

## Content inspiration and research

### Kleo — free creator research and content inspiration
**Free: completely free Chrome extension**

[Kleo](https://kleo.so) is a Chrome extension that lets you view any LinkedIn creator's best-performing posts filtered by engagement rate, date range, and post type. You visit any profile on LinkedIn, open Kleo, and see their top content ranked by performance rather than recency.

It is the fastest way to research what works in your niche, study creators you want to learn from, and find formats worth adapting. The post formatting and preview tools are also included free.

**Best for:** Creators who want to research what content performs well before investing time writing their own.

**Get it:** [kleo.so](https://kleo.so) — completely free.

All free, no login needed for most tools.

## Post scheduling

### LinkedIn native scheduler — free built-in scheduling
**Free: built into LinkedIn**

LinkedIn's own post scheduler is free and lets you schedule posts up to three months in advance directly inside the platform. Click the clock icon in the post composer to access it. It covers the core scheduling use case for most individual creators without requiring any third-party tool.

**Best for:** Anyone who wants to batch-write posts and schedule them without paying for a tool.

**Get it:** Already in your LinkedIn account — click the clock icon in the post composer.

## LinkedIn's own free tools worth knowing about

LinkedIn itself offers several underused free features:

**Following feed** — visit [linkedin.com/feed/following](https://linkedin.com/feed/following) for a narrower feed showing only accounts you follow rather than the full algorithm feed.

**Native post analytics** — every post shows views, reactions, comments, and basic demographic data of who saw it. Access by clicking View analytics under any post.

**Creator mode** — free profile feature that adds a Follow button, enables LinkedIn Live access, and shows your featured topics on your profile. Worth enabling if you post regularly.

**LinkedIn newsletters** — free built-in newsletter tool that notifies all your followers when you publish. Higher open rates than email for most LinkedIn audiences.
