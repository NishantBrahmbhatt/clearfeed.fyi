## What are AICarousels' limitations?

**It is web-only.** There is no mobile app and no desktop application. You need a browser and a reasonably sized screen to work comfortably. For most LinkedIn creators that's fine, carousel creation is mostly a desktop activity anyway, but it's still worth knowing.

**The AI writing needs editing.** The AI generates a solid first draft, but it's still a first draft. The slides will be competent and well-structured, but they won't sound like you without editing. Plan for 10 to 15 minutes of personalisation on top of the generation time. That's actually a feature, not a bug, because you want the final output to reflect your voice and expertise, not a generic AI version of the topic.

**The free plan has daily limits.** If you want to batch-create multiple carousels in one sitting, you will hit the free tier cap and need to upgrade or wait. For occasional use the free plan is fine. For consistent weekly carousel creation the Pro plan is the right call.
