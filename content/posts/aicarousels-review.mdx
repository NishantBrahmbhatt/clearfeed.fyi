## Why do LinkedIn carousels matter so much right now?

Skip this section and the "buy a carousel tool" pitch makes no sense. Stay for thirty seconds and it does.

A Metricool analysis of over 670,000 LinkedIn posts found carousels achieving 11 times more interactions than individual images, with a 49.52% engagement rate and an average of over 600 interactions and clicks per post.

Socialinsider's analysis of 1.3 million LinkedIn posts found native documents (the carousel format) at the top for engagement: a 7% average engagement rate and a 14% year-on-year increase in performance.

Buffer adds the behavioural layer: people linger roughly 15 to 20 seconds on carousels versus 8 to 10 on single image or text. LinkedIn cares about dwell. More seconds, more distribution.

The format works because each swipe is a tiny yes. By the last slide the reader has invested real time. The algorithm reads that as quality. You read it as attention earned.

The problem was never the format. It was the labour.

## What are AICarousels' limitations?

**Web only.** No mobile app, no desktop binary. Plan on a real screen.

**AI copy is a first draft.** Structure will be fine. Voice will be generic until you edit. Budget 10 to 15 minutes to sound like you. That is not a flaw; publishing raw model tone is.

**Free caps.** Batch several decks in one night and you will wait or upgrade. Occasional use: free is fine. Weekly carousel habit: Pro is the realistic tier.

**Platform scope.** Built for LinkedIn, Instagram, and TikTok workflows. <Citation source="AIChief" href="https://aichief.com/ai-marketing-tools/aicarousels/" year="2025" /> If you live on X or Facebook first, this is not your primary hammer.
