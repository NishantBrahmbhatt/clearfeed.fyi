Managing your LinkedIn feed effectively comes down to using the right tool for the right problem. Some people need fewer distractions. Others need a reliable way to follow specific people. Others care most about scheduling and analytics. No single tool does everything well, so here's how the main options compare in 2025.

## What are the main LinkedIn feed management tools available in 2025?

Five tools cover the majority of LinkedIn feed management use cases in 2025: MyFeedIn, Taplio, Shield, Minimal LinkedIn, and LinkedIn's own native features. Each solves a different problem and targets a different type of user.

Understanding which problem you are actually trying to solve is more useful than picking a tool based on feature lists alone.

## Taplio — best for content scheduling and AI writing

Taplio is a LinkedIn content tool focused on the publishing side rather than the consumption side. It offers AI-assisted post writing, content scheduling, post analytics, and a feed of viral LinkedIn content for inspiration.

Taplio does include a feed feature that lets you follow specific creators, but this is not its primary focus and is less refined than MyFeedIn's custom feed functionality.

**Best for:** LinkedIn creators who post frequently and want AI assistance writing posts, a scheduling queue, and detailed performance analytics in one tool.

**Price:** Starts at approximately $49 per month <Citation source="Taplio" href="https://taplio.com" year="2025" />. No meaningful free plan.

**Limitation:** Expensive for users who only need feed management. The AI writing features are the main value driver, so if you don't need them, the cost is hard to justify.

## Shield — best for post analytics

Shield is a dedicated LinkedIn analytics tool that tracks the performance of your own posts in significant detail. It shows views, engagement rates, follower growth, and content breakdowns by post type over time, with better historical data and charting than LinkedIn's native analytics.

Shield does not manage your feed or help you find content to engage with. It is purely an analytics product for understanding how your own content performs.

**Best for:** Established LinkedIn creators who post regularly and want data to guide their content strategy.

**Price:** Starts at approximately $8 per month <Citation source="MyFeedIn Blog" href="https://myfeedin.co/blog/10-best-chrome-extensions-for-linkedin-2026" year="2026" />.

**Limitation:** Analytics only. Does not help with feed management, engagement, or content creation.

## Minimal LinkedIn — best free distraction remover

Minimal LinkedIn is a free Chrome extension that strips distractions from LinkedIn's interface, removing the news feed, notifications panel, and other attention-grabbing elements, so LinkedIn becomes mostly a search and messaging tool.

It does not build custom feeds or let you follow specific people. It simply removes the elements of LinkedIn most likely to cause unintentional scrolling.

**Best for:** People who use LinkedIn primarily for search and messaging and want to remove the feed without replacing it with anything.

**Price:** Free.

**Limitation:** No custom feed functionality. You cannot use it to engage with specific people's content.

## LinkedIn native features — what the platform actually offers

LinkedIn's own tools for feed management are limited but worth knowing about:

**Following feed.** Accessible at linkedin.com/feed/following, this shows content only from people you follow rather than the full algorithm feed. It still runs through the algorithm and cannot be filtered to specific people, but it is narrower than the default feed.

**Unfollow without disconnecting.** You can unfollow any connection to remove their posts from your feed while keeping the connection intact. Aggressive unfollowing shrinks the pool the algorithm draws from.

**Profile notifications.** Clicking the bell icon on any LinkedIn profile sends you a notification every time that person posts. Useful for one or two key people but impractical at scale.

None of these native features approach the control that a dedicated tool like MyFeedIn provides.
