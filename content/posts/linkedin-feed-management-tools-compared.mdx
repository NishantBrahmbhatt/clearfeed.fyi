“Best feed tool” is the wrong first question.

First question: **what is broken for you?** Noise? Missing people you follow? Analytics? Publishing? Pick the job, then pick the wrench.

## What are the main LinkedIn feed management tools available in 2025?

Five names cover most sane workflows: **MyFeedIn**, **Taplio**, **Shield**, **Minimal LinkedIn**, and **LinkedIn itself** (Following tab, unfollow, bells).

None of them is a Swiss Army knife. That is fine. You are not trying to buy one hero app. You are trying to solve one sharp pain.

## MyFeedIn — best for custom feeds and targeted engagement

[MyFeedIn](https://myfeedin.co?atp=clearfeed) is the most **single-minded** option: named lists of people, their posts only, inside LinkedIn, without the sideshow.

You also get post analytics, comment → profile analytics, and focus mode that walls off the default feed when you want discipline.

**Best for:** Creators, sellers, anyone who keeps “just checking” and loses an hour.

**Price:** Free for one feed / ten people. Paid unlocks more lists and team workflows.

**Limitation:** Desktop Chromium. No mobile extension.

## Taplio — best for content scheduling and AI writing

Taplio lives on the **publishing** side: AI drafts, queues, analytics, inspiration feeds. It can follow creators, but that is not the spine of the product.

**Best for:** High-volume posters who want one paid cockpit.

**Price:** Roughly **$49/mo** and up <Citation source="Taplio" href="https://taplio.com" year="2025" />. No real free tier.

**Limitation:** If you only want quieter reading, you are overpaying.

## Shield — best for post analytics

Shield charts **your** posts: views, engagement mix, history. It does not reorder what you read.

**Best for:** Established creators optimising format mix.

**Price:** From about **$8/mo** <Citation source="MyFeedIn Blog" href="https://myfeedin.co/blog/10-best-chrome-extensions-for-linkedin-2026" year="2026" />.

**Limitation:** Read-only insight. Zero feed control.

**Minimal LinkedIn — free distraction remover**

[Minimal LinkedIn](https://www.minimallinkedin.com/) strips visual noise. It does **not** build custom lists. Two makers, zero price, sometimes flaky on LinkedIn UI variants.

**Best for:** “Make the page calmer” without changing who surfaces.

**Price:** Free.

## LinkedIn native features — what the platform actually offers

**Following URL** (`linkedin.com/feed/following`): narrower, still ranked.

**Unfollow:** polite mute. Graph stays.

**Bell per profile:** works for one hero account, silly for twenty.

None of that is **list-complete reading**. For that you still go third-party.
